# Days of Milan — Brand Guidelines

Reference doc for all Days of Milan brand work in this repo (UI, copy, marketing assets). Source: "Days of Milan Guidelines 2026" (Nanna & Nee Design Studio).

## Brand Overview

**What it is:** Days of Milan is a companion for anyone navigating Milan — thoughtful recommendations, local insights, and a sense of connection for newcomers and long-time residents alike.

**Vision:** Create a world where anyone in Milan feels at home. A trusted, go-to companion that blends local culture and international experience — natural, inclusive, genuinely human.

**Mission:** Make Milan feel more accessible, enjoyable, and connected through thoughtful, curated content. Guided by simplicity, authenticity, and a real understanding of what it feels like to be new somewhere.

**Audience:** Curious, creative people who want to experience the city more deeply — looking for hidden gems, meaningful moments, and a sense of belonging in a new place.

### Core Values

- **Simplicity** — exploring the city should feel easy and enjoyable. Everything shared is clear, thoughtful, uncomplicated.
- **Authentic** — no overhyped recommendations or fluff. Honest, carefully chosen places and experiences that genuinely reflect the city.
- **Connected** — built on bringing people together, creating belonging, turning a big city into a shared experience.
- **Curious** — encourages looking beyond the obvious: hidden corners, local favourites, a deeper way of experiencing Milan.
- **Meaningful Experiences** — everything is rooted in intention; not just places, but how those places make people feel.

## Logo

- **Primary logo:** "DAYS OF MiLAN" wordmark, stacked two lines. The "O" in "OF" is replaced with a location pin. An eight-point star (compass/direction motif) sits behind the pin. Font is strong, slightly rough, rounded, mixed case (note the lowercase "i" in "MiLAN") — human and easygoing, not corporate.
- **Secondary logo:** Same wordmark, flat (no star), used when the starburst is impractical (small sizes, tight layouts, boxed lockups).
- **Icon:** The eight-point star + location pin alone, used as a standalone mark/favicon/social avatar. Available in Sky, Merlot, Night, and Ivory colorways.
- **Safe zone:** Clear space around the logo equal to the height of one letter ("x") on all sides — nothing else should enter this zone.

### Logo Usage — Approved Colorways

- Merlot logo on Sky background
- Sky logo on Merlot background
- Night logo on Sky background
- Sky (tinted/ghosted) logo on Ivory background
- Merlot logo on Sky background
- Ivory logo on Merlot background
- Sky logo on Sky (tone-on-tone) background
- Sky logo on Night background

Rule of thumb: logo must always have strong contrast against its background, using only brand colors (Sky, Merlot, Ivory, Night).

### Logo Usage — Do NOT

- Don't split/crop the wordmark (e.g. losing the "O" pin)
- Don't recolor the logo in non-brand colors or mismatch the icon color to the wordmark color
- Don't outline-only the wordmark
- Don't add drop shadows/outlines/effects not in the approved set
- Don't use low-contrast combinations (e.g. dark text on dark background, light on light)
- Don't place the tinted/ghost version anywhere but Ivory background

## Colours

Palette inspired by the feeling of Milan: soft light, warm tones, a balance of calm and character. Sky and Merlot lead (personality, contrast, recognisability); Ivory and Night support (softness and structure).

| Name | Hex | RGB | CMYK | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sky** | `#BCD2E0` | 188, 210, 224 | C16 M9 Y0 K12 | Primary brand colour. Light, calm, open — friendly/approachable tone, reflects exploration and ease. |
| **Merlot** | `#840808` | 132, 8, 8 | C0 M94 Y94 K48 | Key accent. Warmth, depth, character. Contrast and richness for elements that need to stand out. |

## Typography

- **Primary typeface: Kopiku** — used for headings, titles, standout messaging. Strong, slightly rough, bold, recognisable but approachable/human. This is the main voice of the brand — use consistently for impact and recognition. Typically set in Merlot.
- **Secondary typeface: Helvetica**
  - **Helvetica Bold (all caps)** — subheadings, structure and clarity.
  - **Helvetica Regular** — all body copy, longer paragraphs, supporting content, readability and balance.

Together Kopiku (expressive) + Helvetica (neutral) = expressive-but-legible brand voice; Helvetica supports without competing with Kopiku.

## Imagery / Photography Style

- Real, lived-in moments capturing the feeling of discovering a city.
- Centred on people, places, everyday experiences — natural and unposed.
- Light, welcoming, full of atmosphere; balance of clean composition and authentic detail.
- Colour/tone: soft, slightly editorial, natural light, subtle warmth.
- Overall feel: human, calm, connected — belonging and discovery.
- Example subjects: street scenes, aperitivo/food close-ups, neighbourhood shopfronts, casual outdoor gatherings — not staged or overly polished.

## Applying This to the Codebase

- Use Sky/Merlot/Ivory/Night as the CSS colour palette (see hex values above) — don't introduce off-brand colours.
- Headings/titles → Kopiku; body copy and UI text → Helvetica (Bold caps for subheadings, Regular for body).
- Copy/microcopy tone: casual, warm, friendly, simple — avoid corporate or stiff phrasing.
- Any logo/icon usage in the UI must follow the approved colorway combinations and safe-zone spacing above.
